II PAUSED · FIELD GUIDE

  

   
Explore → fight → improve → descend. Walk into an enemy to fight. Find $ vaults for supplies and § merchants for permanent upgrades. The Warden and King seal the stairs on floors 5 and 10.

   
Select two neighbouring tiles to make a line of three. Invalid swaps are free. A straight line of 4+ grants an extra turn, including cascades; one bonus per move. Against ordinary enemies, a bonus turn cannot earn another bonus turn; bosses can still chain them. Cascades increase match strength.

   

    
♠ ATTACKWeapon damage. Your attack and skill boost matter; enemy armour reduces it.

    
◆ DEFENCEGain shield. It absorbs hits and lasts for this fight.

    
★ MAGICSpend MP for armour-piercing damage. Low MP means a weaker attack.

    
♥ HEALTHRestore HP, up to your maximum.

    
♣ SKILLRestore MP and boost weapon damage for this fight. A line of 4+ also prepares a dodge.

   

   
Story mode: The Last Account. Follow ten short chapters to the Forgotten King. Your choices grant supplies or preserve records; collect at least two of the three register pages to unlock the restoration ending. J opens your journal between fights. Story mode autosaves at safe points; Continue appears on the title screen. Death still ends the run and deletes that checkpoint. C starts Classic without story scenes; E starts Endless.

   
Build your run. Choose a permanent relic on floors 1, 4 and 7. Equip two of four spells; change them between fights with B. In combat, press 1 or 2 to cast instead of matching. Board Shift is the exception: once per fight, it refreshes the board and keeps your turn. Match ♣ to recharge your MP.

   
Relics trigger automatically. Match effects apply in combat. Poison and burn ignore armour and tick before the enemy acts. Reapplying either refreshes its duration; it does not add extra turns of damage.

   
Read NEXT. The enemy will use that move. Shields still block piercing attacks; piercing bypasses your passive defence. Dodge avoids a damaging hit and its effects. STUN skips one player turn automatically.

   
Vaults: ♠ gold · ◆ shield for your next fight · ★ MP · ♥ HP · ♣ +0.5 gold multiplier, capped at ×3. Match 4+ to keep your vault move. Floor 1 vault gold is capped at 70G and floor 2 at 100G; later vaults are uncapped.

   
WASD / arrows to move · Enter / Space to select · Esc cancels a selected tile, otherwise pauses · . to wait in the dungeon · click, tap or swipe on a board. T rerolls shop stock; Esc / Q leaves the shop.
